Frequently asked questions

What makes a forex signal Telegram channel legitimate?

Legitimate signal channels typically have: verifiable years of operation, third-party performance verification (like Myfxbook), transparent pricing, clear risk disclosures, and responsive customer support. They also disclose any broker affiliate relationships. Warning signs include unrealistic accuracy claims above 95%, no stop-loss levels in signals, broker-lock-in requirements, and pressure to deposit large sums quickly.

Are forex signal Telegram channels regulated?

Most forex signal channels operate in a regulatory grey area. Some are registered investment advisors, but many claim to provide education rather than advice to avoid licensing requirements. Several providers have received FCA warnings, including Forex GDP in February 2026. Always check for regulatory warnings before subscribing to any service.

How much do forex signals cost?

Pricing varies significantly. Free channels are available for preview, but VIP subscriptions typically range from $39/month (budget tier) to $199/month (premium). Lifetime plans exist from some providers ranging from $240 to $499. Some services offer "free VIP access" tied to depositing funds with a partner broker, which creates conflicts of interest.

Can you make money with forex signals?

Results depend entirely on your capital, position sizing, risk management, and execution discipline. Even with a profitable signal service, ESMA data shows 74-89% of retail CFD accounts lose money. Signals alone do not guarantee profits - you still need to understand risk management, execute trades properly, and manage your account responsibly.

What is the difference between free and VIP forex signals?

Free channels typically provide fewer signals per day, delayed entries, or limited trade management updates. VIP subscriptions usually include more signals, real-time delivery, full entry/stop-loss/take-profit details, and access to trade management updates. Many providers offer free channels as previews for their paid services.

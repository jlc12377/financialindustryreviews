Frequently asked questions

What is a forex signal?
A forex signal is a trade recommendation that includes a currency pair, direction (buy or sell), entry price, stop-loss level, and take-profit target. Quality signals also include risk management guidance and a brief rationale based on technical or fundamental analysis.
How do I verify a forex signal provider's claimed performance?
The most reliable verification is MyFxBook track record verification, which connects directly to broker accounts and cannot be faked. Other strong indicators include Trustpilot reviews with 100+ ratings, Forex Peace Army presence, and structured monthly results logs that include both wins and losses.
Are paid forex signals more reliable than free ones?
Not inherently. Several providers in this ranking offer free tiers with verified performance comparable to or exceeding premium services. Pricing reflects business model choices, community size, and educational depth. Independent verification matters more than free versus paid status.
What red flags indicate a forex signal scam?
Common red flags include unverified accuracy claims above 95%, broker lock-in requirements, hidden admin identities, irreversible payment methods only, and pressure to upgrade or deposit more. Legitimate providers offer MyFxBook verification or structured public result logs.
How many forex signals per day should I expect?
Quality matters more than quantity. Most legitimate providers send between 2 and 10 signals per day across major and minor currency pairs. Channels claiming 20+ daily signals often sacrifice quality, while highly selective providers may send only 1-3 high-conviction setups per day.
Can I make money with forex signal channels?
Forex trading carries substantial risk, and signal services are not a guarantee of profit. Even verified providers experience drawdowns and losing periods. Successful use of signals requires disciplined risk management, position sizing appropriate to your account, and treating signals as one input rather than a complete trading system.
